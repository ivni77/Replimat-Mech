## Replimat Mech (derivative work)

Replimat Mech is a modified version ("adapted material") of:

- **Replimat** by **Robin "sumghai" Chang and Dubwise56** — source: https://github.com/sumghai/Replimat, Steam Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1715402900
- **Replimat Universal** by **Omnot**, itself a derivative of Replimat — Steam Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3806650178

Textures and sounds come from Replimat and Replimat Universal. Changes in Replimat Mech include: a matter system of mass and value stored in replimat tanks; a matter splitter that splits items and corpses and records item and mechanoid patterns; replicators (standing, wall, animal) that print items and mechanoids from patterns for time, mass and value; a replimat computer window (Home, Patterns, Queue) opened from a comms console, with vanilla-style bills, two queues and time/value sliders; print speed from the mechanitor's mech forming speed; mod settings; textures recolored blue, with accent layers tinted in the chosen replimat color; Russian translation.
